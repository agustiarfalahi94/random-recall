Privacy policy — English

1. Who is responsible

Random Recall is made by Agustiar Falahi, an independent developer publishing as Inkpebble Studio, based in Indonesia. For anything in this policy, including data requests, write to agustiarfalahi@gmail.com.

2. Using the app without an account

Signing in is optional. Without an account, your questions, answers, categories, practice history, streaks, reminder schedule and settings live in a local database on your device and are not uploaded anywhere. A random installation identifier is kept on the device so the app knows which local data belongs to which signed-in user if you sign in later; it is not a person's identity and is never used for advertising.

3. What an account stores

If you choose to sign in — with email and password, with Google, or with a phone number — we store in Google Cloud Firestore, under your own user ID:

  • Your display name, and the email address or phone number you signed in with.
  • The questions, answers and categories you create.
  • Practice records, answer statistics, streaks and challenge progress.
  • Account timestamps such as when the profile was created or last updated.

Signing in with Google shares your name, email address and account identifier with the app; signing in with a phone number shares that number. Your password, when you use one, is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us. Your data is readable only by your own account: the database rules are written so that no other user, and no anonymous request, can read or write it.

4. Advertising

Random Recall shows a banner ad so that the app can stay free. It uses Google AdMob, which may collect your advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from that address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. That data is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and applicable US states, a Google consent form asks before any personalised advertising. You can change or withdraw that choice later in the app under Settings → Ad privacy settings, which appears wherever a consent choice applies.
  • If you decline personalised ads, you still see ads, but they are not selected from your advertising ID.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Uninstalling the app stops all app-related advertising immediately.

5. Diagnostics and usage statistics

The app uses Google Firebase for diagnostics and product decisions:

  • Analytics — events such as opening the app, answering a question (whether it was correct, how long it took, whether it came from a notification, hour of day), creating a question, and signing in or out. When signed in, events are associated with your user ID.
  • Crashlytics — crash and error reports with the technical stack trace, device model, OS version and app version, plus your user ID when signed in, so a broken account can be traced.
  • Performance Monitoring — timings and network request health, without content.
  • Remote Config — fetches non-personal configuration values such as feature flags and copy.
  • App Check — proves that requests come from a genuine copy of the app installed from Google Play, using Google Play Integrity on your device.

The text of your questions and answers is never included in analytics events or crash reports. Firebase Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months; Crashlytics reports for 90 days.

6. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device itself. The app asks for permission to post notifications, to schedule exact alarms and, optionally, to be exempt from battery optimisation; all three are needed for reminders to arrive on time. Nothing about your questions is sent through a push service to deliver them.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it for advertising outside Google AdMob.
  • We do not read your contacts, calendar, SMS, files or photos.
  • We do not collect precise location, and we do not use the microphone or camera.
  • We do not show third-party ads for products we choose ourselves; ad creative is served by Google.

8. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies: the account and its data are processed to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)); diagnostics and security measures rely on our legitimate interest in a working, abuse-resistant app (Art. 6(1)(f)); personalised advertising rests on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

9. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, or ask for it in a portable format. Most of it you can already see and edit in the app, and you can delete everything yourself with Delete Account. For anything else, email agustiarfalahi@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. If you are in Indonesia, the same rights follow from Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP). You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Retention and deletion

Account data is kept for as long as the account exists. Deleting the account removes the sign-in record and all of its data immediately, and the local database on the device is cleared. Analytics and crash data expire on the schedule in section 5 and are no longer linked to an identifiable account afterwards. Encrypted backups held by Google Cloud may persist for up to 30 days after deletion before they are overwritten.

11. Security

All traffic between the app and Google's services uses TLS. Database and storage rules limit access to the signed-in owner. App Check with Play Integrity blocks requests from modified or automated clients. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your device lock and your account password in good shape.

12. International transfers

Google's infrastructure may process data outside Indonesia, including in the United States, under Google's standard contractual clauses and its own data processing terms. Data is transferred only to Google as described here, never to anyone else.

13. Children

Random Recall is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If a child has created an account, write to us and the account will be deleted.

14. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, analytics, crash reporting, performance, remote config, app integrity.
  • Google AdMob — advertising and the consent form for it.
  • Google Play services — sign-in and app integrity checks on Android.

Each is a Google service governed by Google's privacy policy.

15. Changes to this policy

If how the app handles data changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves. Material changes are also noted in the app's release notes, so a new version never arrives with a quietly different policy.

1. Siapa yang bertanggung jawab

Random Recall dibuat oleh Agustiar Falahi, pengembang independen yang menerbitkan aplikasi sebagai Inkpebble Studio, berdomisili di Indonesia. Untuk semua hal dalam kebijakan ini, termasuk permintaan data, hubungi agustiarfalahi@gmail.com.

2. Memakai aplikasi tanpa akun

Masuk akun sepenuhnya opsional. Tanpa akun, pertanyaan, jawaban, kategori, riwayat latihan, streak, jadwal pengingat, dan pengaturan tersimpan di basis data lokal di perangkatmu dan tidak diunggah ke mana pun. Ada identifier pemasangan acak yang disimpan di perangkat supaya aplikasi tahu data lokal mana milik pengguna yang sedang masuk kalau kamu memutuskan masuk akun nanti; itu bukan identitas orang dan tidak pernah dipakai untuk iklan.

3. Apa yang disimpan saat punya akun

Kalau kamu memilih masuk — dengan email dan kata sandi, dengan Google, atau dengan nomor HP — kami menyimpan di Google Cloud Firestore, di bawah ID penggunamu sendiri:

  • Nama tampilan, dan alamat email atau nomor HP yang kamu pakai untuk masuk.
  • Pertanyaan, jawaban, dan kategori yang kamu buat.
  • Catatan latihan, statistik jawaban, streak, dan progres tantangan.
  • Waktu pencatatan akun, misalnya kapan profil dibuat atau terakhir diperbarui.

Masuk dengan Google membagikan nama, alamat email, dan identifier akun ke aplikasi; masuk dengan nomor HP membagikan nomor itu. Kata sandi, kalau kamu memakainya, ditangani Firebase Authentication dan tidak pernah terlihat oleh kami. Datamu hanya bisa dibaca oleh akunmu sendiri: aturan basis data ditulis supaya pengguna lain maupun permintaan anonim tidak bisa membacanya.

4. Iklan

Random Recall menampilkan banner iklan supaya aplikasi bisa tetap gratis. Iklan disediakan Google AdMob, yang dapat mengumpulkan ID iklanmu, alamat IP, perkiraan lokasi dari alamat itu, informasi perangkat dan aplikasi, serta interaksimu dengan iklan. Data tersebut diproses Google sesuai kebijakan privasi Google.

  • Di Kawasan Ekonomi Eropa, Inggris, Swiss, dan negara bagian AS yang berlaku, formulir izin Google muncul sebelum iklan yang dipersonalisasi. Kamu bisa mengubah atau menarik pilihan itu kapan saja di aplikasi melalui Pengaturan → Pengaturan privasi iklan, yang muncul di mana pilihan izin memang berlaku.
  • Kalau kamu menolak iklan yang dipersonalisasi, iklan tetap tampil, tetapi tidak dipilih berdasarkan ID iklanmu.
  • ID iklan bisa diatur ulang atau dihapus kapan saja di Pengaturan Android → Privasi → Iklan. Menghapus aplikasi langsung menghentikan seluruh iklan terkait aplikasi.

5. Diagnostik dan statistik pemakaian

Aplikasi memakai Google Firebase untuk diagnostik dan keputusan produk:

  • Analytics — peristiwa seperti membuka aplikasi, menjawab pertanyaan (benar atau salah, berapa lama, dari notifikasi atau tidak, jam berapa), membuat pertanyaan, serta masuk atau keluar akun. Saat sudah masuk, peristiwa dikaitkan dengan ID penggunamu.
  • Crashlytics — laporan crash dan error bersama jejak teknis, model perangkat, versi OS dan versi aplikasi, plus ID pengguna saat masuk, supaya akun yang bermasalah bisa ditelusuri.
  • Performance Monitoring — durasi dan kesehatan permintaan jaringan, tanpa konten.
  • Remote Config — mengambil nilai konfigurasi non-pribadi seperti flag fitur dan teks.
  • App Check — membuktikan permintaan datang dari salinan aplikasi asli yang dipasang dari Google Play, memakai Google Play Integrity di perangkatmu.

Teks pertanyaan dan jawabanmu tidak pernah disertakan dalam peristiwa analitik atau laporan crash. Data Firebase Analytics disimpan sampai 14 bulan; laporan Crashlytics 90 hari.

6. Notifikasi

Pengingat dijadwalkan langsung di perangkatmu. Aplikasi meminta izin menampilkan notifikasi, menjadwalkan alarm presisi, dan opsional dikecualikan dari optimasi baterai; ketiganya diperlukan agar pengingat datang tepat waktu. Tidak ada isi pertanyaanmu yang dikirim lewat layanan push untuk mengantarkannya.

  • Kami tidak membaca kontak, kalender, SMS, file, atau foto.

Backup terenkripsi milik Google Cloud dapat bertahan sampai 30 hari setelah penghapusan sebelum tertimpa.
